principles
fonts
have three categories: primary are personal preferences, secondary are safe fallbacks, and experimental are fun
avoid played out fonts like Futura, Helvetica, and Times New Roman
colors
have four axes (hue, name, tone, shade)
hue is one of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, brown, black, gray, or white
name is a short word from earth or nature, grouped within a family (soil, bark, nut)
tone is one of natural (muted, earthy) or vivid (saturated)
shade is one of primary, secondary, or tertiary, going from dark to light
hexes are memorable wherever the color allows, such as #AABBCC, #606060, #EFFEEE, or palindrome #DFEEFD
