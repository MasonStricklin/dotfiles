principles
fonts
primary are personal favorites, secondary are safe and experimental are fun
avoid what has been played out (Futura, Helvetica, Times New Roman)
colors
are points on four axes (hue, name, tone, shade)
names are one short word from earth or nature, ordered within a family (soil, bark, nut)
hexes are memorable wherever the color allows, ideally doubled #AABBCC, gray #606060, two digits #EFFEEE or palindrome #DFEEFD, and failing that round #FFD0BB, same last digit #695949 or two equal channels #FAF2FA (these examples are not exhaustive)
